Players are ranked by their average finish across the heroes they play, each finish weighted by how much it's worth trusting: a deeper pool counts for more (#1 of 20 over #1 of 3, saturating around 7 opponents — past that, deeper fields in this group are not stronger ones), and a hero you've actually played counts for more than one that only just cleared the display floor. Finishing position itself is never rewritten by pool size — 7th of 36 is 7th of 36. That average is then pulled toward the middle of the board by how much evidence backs it, so breadth and volume decide who leads when two records are close — but a thin record is only ever held back from the top, never pushed below a worse one. The Avg finish chip on each profile is the raw, unadjusted figure. The standing itself is an exact place — players who score equally are separated by their average finish, so no two share one. A player carrying less than one fully-credible hero's worth of evidence is shown with a — instead of a place: not enough to rank them, which is not the same as a low rank.

How the crown and the projected rank are decided

The goes to the group's best player on that hero by a multi-method consensus, over the players past this mode's game floor — that count is the Pool. Margin is the gap to the runner-up on the Z-Score composite; at or under 3 the crown reads Contested. With only two players there is no spread to measure: the margin reduces to which stats the winner leads on, not by how much, so read those as unranked rather than dominant.

Projected rank is not anyone's real rank, and is not built from it. It starts from what the player produces against the lobbies they actually get, then moves per hero by how far they out-perform that hero's own typical standing — so playing a strong hero averagely does not read as skill. Their own output caps it: a hero cannot read high on dominance alone. A hero with no dominance sample sits at the player's overall level.

Switching Quick Match / Competitive re-decides the crown, not the rank. The projection is a property of that player on that hero, so the column does not move when you change lens — while the winner beside it very often does.
