How the standing is earned

On each hero, a player's result is the share of the other tracked players on that hero they finish above: 7th of 36 beats 29 of the other 35. The standing averages that share across their heroes, trusting a hero more when there are more players to beat on it (up to six) and when the player has more games on it.

That average is then pulled toward the middle of the board by how much evidence backs it, so a broad, well-played record leads when two records are close — and a thin record is only ever held back from the top, never pushed below a worse one.

Avg finish is the same share as a plain average, printed as where they usually place: Top 11% is ahead of 89% of each hero's players, 1st is first on every hero. The standing is an exact place: an exact tie breaks on Avg finish. A — instead of a place means too little evidence to rank yet, not a low rank.

How these tables are ranked

Every leaderboard here is ordered by a sample-shrunk win rate, not by the rate it prints. The shrink pulls each row toward that map's own average by 25 pseudo-games, so a thin run cannot top the board on luck: 8 games keeps 24% · 20 games keeps 44% · 50 games keeps 67% · 200 games keeps 89% of its own rate, and the rest is the map average. The printed Win % stays the raw record — hover it for the shrunk figure the order actually used.

The bar follows the printed rate, not the order — so a longer bar can sit below a shorter one, and that is the shrink doing its job on a thin run rather than a sorting fault. The bar is drawn against a window of ±25 points around 50%, so ordinary differences are visible instead of every row reading half-full; rates past that window draw a full or empty bar.

Games counts one tracked player in one match, so a match with three of us in it counts three times. A hero's games are the matches where it was that player's main pick.

Three floors: a row needs 8+ games to be listed, the drill-down hides heroes and players under 4, and a map is only featured at all once the squad has 20+ games on it.
